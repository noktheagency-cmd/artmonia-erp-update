# Student attendance overview

The Students overview includes the **Retention and Churn Rate** card and an Azerbaijani dialog, loaded through `src/modules/students/attendance-analytics-action.ts`. It is read-only and does not finalize sessions or write attendance. Program and teacher distribution cards display all returned categories, with scrolling for long lists.

## Retention and churn

- The dashboard churn card links to `/students?tab=overview&attendance=1` with `attendanceFrom` and `attendanceTo`, opening this same dialog. The dashboard defaults to the current Baku calendar month through today and passes its selected date filter; future endpoints are capped at today.
- Both dashboard and dialog use `retention-analytics-sql.ts`. Churn counts distinct students who entered **FROZEN** or **STOPPED** during the inclusive selected period. DECLINED is excluded. Explicit freeze-period starts also count; duplicate history and freeze-period records count once per student/status, and students in both categories count once in the combined total. Later reactivation does not erase an event from the selected period.
- The denominator is non-test students registered on or before the period end, within the same access and teacher/group filters. This is a period event share of the registered student base, not an opening-active-cohort churn rate. Retention is its complement. An empty base displays 0% churn. Existing frozen/stopped statuses without a dated event in the period do not count as a new loss.
- Clicking frozen/stopped cards opens a detail table in the same dialog: student profile, latest matching date, reason/note and responsible manager. Detail rows paginate at 20, while totals aggregate the entire filtered set.
- Student and attendance scopes intersect for both summary and details; dashboard cache keys include actor and both scopes. Teacher/group membership uses date-overlapping schedule assignments (including the day before the start, to retain students paused on the first day); enrollments without any schedule assignment use their assigned teacher. Teacher/group options also include students without attendance records. Historical slot teacher edits can affect this reconstruction.
- Retention date ranges are independent of the 93-day attendance limit. Longer dashboard ranges still show retention details; the attendance section asks for a narrower range. Weekday enum labels and date-picker controls are Azerbaijani. User-authored group names, reasons and notes are preserved.
- Regression checks: `retention-analytics.test.ts`. The optional `RETENTION_TEST_DATABASE_URL` check runs SELECT-only synthetic CTE fixtures, without reading or writing application records.

## Attendance

- Both `student.read` and `attendance.read` are required. ASSIGNED student access restricts records to the student's manager; ASSIGNED attendance access restricts records to the teacher linked to the actor. These restrictions intersect. No shared cache is used.
- Default period: the current Baku calendar month through today. Custom periods are inclusive, at most 93 days, with no future dates. Comparison uses the immediately preceding equal number of calendar days.
- Source: ended, non-cancelled `SlotSession` rows, with date-effective schedule assignments and enrollments. Existing attendance's teacher takes precedence over the slot's current teacher; unmarked rows use the current slot teacher. Sessions without an identifiable teacher are excluded. Standalone legacy `Lesson` reservations are not attendance journal records and are not included.
- Historical frozen, reserved, stopped, declined and completed lifecycle periods and explicit student freeze periods are excluded. Freeze end dates and assignment end dates are inclusive. With no prior lifecycle event, assignment eligibility is used rather than applying today's status retroactively.
- Attendance percentage is PRESENT / (PRESENT + ABSENT), rounded to a whole percent; no denominator displays a dash. EXCUSED and unmarked records are counted separately. School totals are weighted by student-lessons. Automatic ABSENT marks remain absences.
- Expected participation counts all eligible student-lessons, including excused and unmarked. Completion is marked / expected. Incomplete lessons count distinct sessions with at least one unmarked eligible record.
- Risk uses one shared rule: two consecutive latest ABSENT student-lessons with the same teacher are warning/yellow; three or more are danger/red. PRESENT, EXCUSED and unmarked lessons break the sequence. Current FROZEN, STOPPED, DECLINED and COMPLETED students are excluded. A student with risk under multiple teachers is counted once in school totals, at their highest risk.
- The **current risky students** metric in the overview and churn dialog receives the exact same server snapshot as the Students risk panel, including yellow/red counts and the IDs opened by its detail link. The snapshot covers the latest 93 inclusive Baku calendar dates through today, across all records the actor may access. It is independent of the dialog's historical date, teacher and group filters, and its date range is displayed explicitly. It remains available when the selected historical attendance range is too long or the attendance report fails.
- Teacher-table risk counts and the teacher risk-only toggle apply the same two/three-absence rule to the selected report period and teacher/group filters. The table labels this as period risk; last attendance is also limited to that period. These historical details may differ from the separately labeled current snapshot.
- The student list risk badges and panel enforce the same intersecting `student.read` manager scope and `attendance.read` teacher scope as attendance analytics. Actors without attendance access receive no risk records.
- Teacher and group filters operate on the authorized report. Teacher drill-down shows group rates and student histories, with 20 students per page and a risk-only toggle. Student links use the existing protected profile route.
- The combined current and comparison query is capped at 20,001 rows. Above 20,000 it returns an explicit request to narrow the period instead of presenting incomplete totals.

Historical limitation: schedule assignments and slot teacher/name edits affect reconstruction when a historical attendance record does not exist. Lessons never materialized as slot sessions cannot be counted as missing journal records.

Regression coverage: `src/modules/students/attendance-analytics.test.ts`.
